What we do

Repaired, not replaced

A lot of worn-out clothes are one broken part away from years more wear.

  • Zips replaced on trousers, jackets, skirts and bags
  • Seams repaired and reinforced at stress points
  • Linings replaced in coats, blazers and jackets
  • Small tears, snags and moth holes mended
  • Buttons, hooks, snaps and clasps replaced
  • Patches on denim and workwear

How tailoring works

  1. 01

    Drop off

    Bring it in during opening hours, or send a photo on WhatsApp first.

  2. 02

Restoration

Older and inherited pieces

Restoring an older piece is about keeping what makes it worth keeping: the cloth, the construction, the history. We look at it first and tell you what is realistic before any cost is discussed.

  • Heirloom and vintage garments: fabric stabilised and repaired
  • Linings and structural repairs on older coats and suits
  • Moth holes and small tears mended
  • Buttons, lace and trim repaired, or matched as closely as we can, colour included
  • Inherited and borrowed wedding dresses

We can stabilise a piece and keep its age, or bring it closer to new. Tell us which you prefer.

Before you bring it in

  • Skip the plastic bag. Wrap it loosely in a clean cotton sheet or pillowcase.
  • Do not treat stains yourself. Tell us what the stain is and roughly how old.
  • Carry it flat or on a hanger, not folded along an old crease.

Questions

About repairs

My zip is stuck, not broken. Does it need replacing?

Often not. A stuck zip can be an alignment problem rather than a worn-out zip. We check which it is before charging for a new one.

Can you mend a tear so it does not show?

On most woven fabrics, yes. Knits and some prints are harder to hide completely; we tell you what to expect for your fabric.

Is an old winter coat worth repairing?

Usually, if the fabric is still sound. A relined coat in a heavier cloth often outlasts a cheap new one.

How do I know if a piece can be restored?

Bring it in or send a photo. We look at the fabric and construction and tell you the options before any work starts.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the job. We confirm a collection date when you bring it in.
