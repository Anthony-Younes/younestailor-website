What “alterations” covers, exactly

Alteration is the broadest word in tailoring, and the easiest to get wrong by guessing instead of measuring. In practice, almost every alteration we do falls into one of a few categories — and most are faster and less expensive than people expect.

  • Hemming — trousers, skirts, dresses, sleeves, shortened or lengthened
  • Taking in or letting out waists, side seams and darts
  • Suit and blazer tapering — sleeves, waist suppression, trouser break
  • Strap shortening and bodice adjustment on dresses
  • Re-cuffing sleeves and trouser legs
  • Zipper, button and snap replacement as part of a fitting
