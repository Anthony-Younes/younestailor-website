Questions

About embroidery

Is there a minimum order?

No. One piece is fine.

Do you charge separately for the design or digitising?

No. You get one all-in price, with no separate design or digitising charge.

Can I see the design before you stitch?

Yes. Ask for a preview and we show it to you before stitching.

What should I send as artwork?

A logo file if you have one. A clear photo of a drawing or handwriting works too. Send it in the WhatsApp chat after the form opens it. If you need help with the artwork, tick the box and we will work on it with you.

Can you stitch on caps?

Yes. Caps are stitched in-house.
