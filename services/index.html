Questions

Choosing a service

My job does not fit any of these. Can you still help?

Probably. Send a message or a photo on WhatsApp and we will tell you if it is something we can do.

Can I combine tailoring and embroidery?

Yes. For example, a hem shortened and a name stitched on the same piece. Mention both when you get in touch.

Do you supply the garments for embroidery?

Yes: tees, hoodies, sweaters, caps, towels and uniforms. You can also bring your own.
