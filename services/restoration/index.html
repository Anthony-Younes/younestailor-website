Careful handling of what can’t be replaced

Restoration is different from a normal repair: the goal isn’t just function, it’s preserving what makes the piece worth keeping in the first place — the construction, the fabric, the history. We treat every heirloom piece this way, not as a faster version of a regular repair.

  • Heirloom and vintage garment restoration — fabric stabilization and repair
  • Wedding dress preservation and restoration, including inherited and borrowed gowns
  • Antique button, lace and trim repair or careful matching
  • Re-lining and structural repair on older coats and suits
  • Honest condition assessment before any cost is discussed
  • Careful handling appropriate to fragile or aged fabric
