Questions

About visiting

Is there parking near the shop?

Call or message us before you drive over and we will tell you the easiest place to park in Blat.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. For a wedding outfit, a large embroidery order or a bigger job, message us first so we can set time aside.

Do you serve customers outside Blat?

Yes. Customers come from Byblos, Amchit, Halat, Fidar and other towns. If you are further away, send a photo first and we will tell you whether the trip is worth it.
