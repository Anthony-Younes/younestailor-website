About us

A new tailor in Blat, Jbeil

Younes Tailor opened in 2025 — a small local workshop focused on doing alterations and repairs properly: measured on the body, finished by hand, with honest advice about what’s worth doing. More about us →

How it works

From drop-off to collection

1

Bring it in or send a photo

Walk in during opening hours, or send a WhatsApp photo for a quick read on what’s needed.

2

We assess and quote

A clear price and timeline before any pin goes in — no surprises at collection.

3

We fit and finish by hand

Pinned on the body where it matters, finished by hand, pressed properly before it leaves.

4

We message you the moment it’s ready — no need to call and check.

Why people choose us

Honest work, fair prices

We’re new, and we’d rather earn a regular customer than win a single job. So we tell you honestly when something isn’t worth altering, quote a fair price before we start, and give every garment — a single button or a wedding suit — the same careful attention.

  • No job too small — a single button gets the same attention as a wedding suit.
  • We tell you honestly when a garment isn’t worth altering, instead of charging anyway.
  • A clear price and timeline up front, confirmed before any work begins.
