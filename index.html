Tailoring

How tailoring works

  1. 01

    Drop off

    Bring the garment in during opening hours. Most jobs need no appointment.

  2. 02

    Fit

    We pin and mark it on you and agree the price before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Bench

    The work is done in the shop, by hand or machine, whichever the seam needs.

  4. 04

    Collect

    We message you when it is ready, pressed.

Embroidery

How embroidery works

  1. 01

    Send item details and artwork

    Tell us the item, the quantity and what to stitch. No artwork yet? Ask for help with it.

  2. 02

    Agree the quote, preview on request

    One price, with no separate design or digitising charge. Ask to see a preview before we stitch.

  3. 03

    Stitch

    We digitise the design in-house and stitch it on our own machine.

  4. 04

    Collect

    We message you when it is ready.

  • No minimum order
  • One price — no separate design or digitising charge
  • Preview before stitching on request

Questions

Before you come in

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during opening hours. For a wedding outfit or a larger job, message us first so we can set time aside.

How long do alterations take?

Most alterations are ready in 2–3 days. Ask when you drop the garment off and we will give you a date.

Do you make clothes from scratch?

Not on the tailoring side: we alter and repair clothes you already own. For embroidery we can supply the blank, including tees, hoodies, sweaters, caps, towels and uniforms.

Is there a minimum order for embroidery?

No. One cap or one towel is fine.

What languages do you speak?

Arabic, French and English.

Where is the shop?

In Blat, a few minutes from Byblos old town. The map and directions are on the Visit page.
